# Low-angle cover review synthesis

User request: original AI Overlord cover with a dominant low-angle smug robot, title, and ruined city, using the supplied New Vegas cover's hierarchy as inspiration. No requirement to show cars. Review only; no new image generated or approved during this pass.

Reviewers: two Codex specialist subagents (art direction/game design and comedy/player interpretation) plus installed Claude Code in safe mode with tools disabled. Claude reviewed a text-only composition brief; the reference attachment was not shared externally. Claude's full response is in CLAUDE-BOXART-REVIEW.md.

Consensus: low viewpoint and title support the power fantasy and permit the expression to change meaning after mastery. Keep restrained closed-mouth smugness, half-lidded eyes/chin slightly raised. A readable work accessory or routine gesture can contrast with the apocalypse and carry comedy. Title, character silhouette and one broad catastrophe shape must survive at 128px.

Strongest critique: this hierarchy also resembles a serious shooter/RPG. Weapons, tactical armor, combat stance, red eyes and grim desaturated rendering would push it toward that genre. Claude particularly challenged heroic combat proportions and favored an ordinary worker gesture.

Disagreement: the comedy reviewer and Claude preferred localized traffic disaster over anonymous city rubble for gameplay specificity. User explicitly proposed a city and optional absence of cars. Synthesis: honor the city concept in the next draft; let the robot's safety vest and a casual traffic-stop hand gesture convey the job, instead of cramming cars back in. Validate what genre viewers expect.

Proposed next brief (pending generation): square illustrated retail-style game cover, AI OVERLORD as a bold upper title, low viewpoint on an original robot worker with quiet superiority, one relaxed stop gesture and orange vest, intact composed foreground against a simplified city collapsing behind. Asymmetric city silhouette and one broad warm smoke/fire shape, cool robot foreground. No copied Fallout design, tagline explaining intent, weapons, panic, evil grin, meme panel border or glossy toy render. Maintain expression large enough to read. Preserve ambiguity: easy job done perfectly while complexity collapses, OR intentional dominance.

Review findings are design judgments, not measured conversion evidence. User approval remains required before adoption.
